# Changelog

## 0.26.0 (2026-10-27)

- A custom career: after the nation, the screen "Your start" chooses the
  age at the start (16 to 32) and the least value of each skill (up to
  70). The challenge starts go to a 1.x release.
- Data packs give only the rows and the columns that they change.
- Data packs can hold the real players and the managers of the clubs
  (`world/Players.txt`), and the crests of the clubs
  (`crests/<club id>.png`). The crest fills the ring of the badge of the
  match, in the perspective of the ring.
- The first community pack: the season 2025/26 for the four English
  leagues, with the names, the kits, the squads of the Premier League and
  the Championship, and original crests (`community/season-2025-26`).
- The name plates and the score bar of the match have clean plates, with
  the names in the middle and no black outline.
- The app has a new icon.

## 0.8.0 (2026-10-27)

- The Life screen opens the casino. Choose a stake from 1 to 50 bux and
  play blackjack, roulette, or the slot machine.
- Blackjack deals from one deck. The dealer stands on 17. A win pays two
  times the stake, and a tie gives the stake back.
- Roulette has a wheel with 0 and 00. Bet on red, black, green, or a
  dozen, and watch the ball fall into a pocket.
- The slot machine pays 3 times the stake for two of the same, and 7 times
  for three.
- The races: bet on one of six horses, at odds from 2/1 to 12/1. With the
  stable from the lifestyle shop, buy horses in the market, train them,
  race them for a prize, and sell them.
- Bets cost energy, and gambling has news: your team mates can ask you to
  the casino, the boss can hear of it, and a player under 18 can be caught.
- More of the marks of New Star Games change: the shirt and the back of
  the cards of the pairs game, the title without the TM, and the names in
  the text ("SSR-" boots and balls).
- The Android app shows the art of the remake.
- The slot reels, the flips of the cards, and other animations with a
  frame range such as "19>" draw as in the original.

## 0.7.0 (2026-10-27)

- The game has its own marks. The title shows the Star Soccer Rebooted
  logo with REBOOTED under it. The TV, the stadium roof, the boards, the
  sponsor, and the newspaper show the SSR marks.
- The match screen shows the night stadium in the TV.
- The Life button opens your relationships with the boss, the team, the
  fans, your partner, and your sponsors. Spend time with them in the
  pairs game to make the relations better.
- Before a match the week has its news: your partner, requests of your
  relations, dilemmas, the style news, borrowed vehicles, club news, and
  tips. Some news asks you a question, and the bars move with your answer.
- After some matches you give an interview. Repeat the order of the green
  buttons to give a good answer.
- The lifestyle shop sells items, vehicles, and properties. They wear
  after each match, and you can repair them. Properties give energy after
  a match. The prices show the bux icon.
- The skills shop sells agents, trainers, boots, and NRG cans. An agent
  makes your pay higher, a trainer gives energy, and boots make your
  skills better in a match.
- Your contract runs out. Talk with the boss in the negotiate game to
  sign a new one. In the transfer window and at the end of a season other
  clubs can make you an offer.
- The news can bring old pro boots, the captaincy, and a sponsor.
- At the end of a season you can retire. The retirement screen shows your
  career and your score. Your first retirement unlocks the hard mode.

## 0.6.0 (2026-10-27)

- The season runs as in the original. Your nation sets up its leagues
  and cups, the continental competitions, and every fourth year the

  rules of the original.
- Play shows the fixtures of your round first. After the match the

- At the end of the season the clubs go up and down. The season review
  shows your place and your counts, and the awards of the player of the
  year come one at a time. Then the next season starts.
- The text of the game has the size, the place, and the shadow of the
  original.
- The prematch screen shows the badges and the plates of the clubs, the
  shield of the competition, the sponsor bar, and the kits. The kit button
  changes the kits.
- The light behind the badges and other gloss images draws as in the
  original.

## 0.5.0 (2026-10-27)

- You can play a match. Press Play on the game menu: the commentary runs,
  the other teams have their chances, and you get your own chances on the
  3D pitch. Drag back from the ball to aim, then tap the big ball to kick.
- The keeper dives, the defenders close you down, and the team-mates make
  their runs. Offside, the wall of a free kick, corners, crosses, headers,
  penalties, and dribbles work as in the original.
- The match has its half time and full time, the energy of the player,
  the work rate and the focus, and the substitutions.
- After a goal you can watch the replay, with the controls of the game:
  play and pause, the timeline, and slow motion when you hold a finger.
- After the match, the match stats give your rating, the changes of your
  relations with the boss, the team, and the fans, and your star rating.
  The weekly update gives the energy of the rest days and your pay in bux.
- Each chance changes the rating of a skill. The ratings screen shows the
  ratings and the type of your player.
- A new career starts with the five trials of the original: technique,
  set pieces, power, dribbling, and vision. Each trial that you pass
  gives a skill level.
- Energy drinks give energy, skill boosts, or relation boosts.

## 0.3.0 (2026-10-27)

- The app runs on iOS and Android. On Android it imports the game that is
  installed on the device. On iOS it imports the file that you put in its
  Documents folder.
- The screens move as in the game. The app runs each screen as a tree of
  elements with the time base of the game: the buttons slide in after
  their delays, the ball rolls in and spins, the clouds move, and the
  floodlights glow.
- A button goes down when you press it. A click is when the touch goes
  up, as in the game. The mouse works as a touch on the desktop.
- Animations, tweens, image sequences, templates, and particle emitters
  work, with the random numbers of the game.
- The layers and the colours mix as in the game. Colours can come from
  the tweaks, so the seats and the banners take the colours of the club.
- Text slants as in the game. The parts of a player image (shirt, pants,
  skin) take the colours of their tweaks.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-27)

- The app draws the main menu of the game from the layout files and the
  image atlases of your copy. It reads the layout language of the game:
  imports, macros, constants, conditions, origins, layers, clips, and the
  arithmetic of the layouts.
- The image atlases of the game work: the key files, the RLE pages, and
  the plain, sliced, and stripped images.
- Text uses the TTF font of the game. A text box wraps and fits its text.
  The text of the English language file fills the tags.
- The app reads the 2025 data of the game: 6138 clubs in 211 nations, and
  1060 competitions.
- The buttons of a screen play their sounds. The app decodes the OGG
  sounds of an APK and the MP3 sounds of an IPA.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-26)

- The app imports the assets from your copy of New Star Soccer: an APK, an
  XAPK, or an IPA. Drop the file on the window, or give its path to the
  app.
